Storm damage in [City]? Start here.

Hail and high wind do damage you often cannot see from the ground. After a storm:

  • Stay off the roof and photograph what you can see from the yard.
  • Look for missing shingles, dented gutters and granules in the downspouts.
  • Catch active leaks and move valuables away from them.
  • Call us for an inspection before you sign anything with a storm chaser.

Roofing questions from [City] homeowners

How much does a new roof cost in [City]?

It depends on the size and pitch of the roof, how many layers come off, the condition of the decking, and the material you choose. We measure the roof and give you a written, itemized price before any work starts.

Should I repair my roof or replace it?

If the damage is limited to one area and the rest of the roof is in good shape, a repair usually makes sense. Widespread granule loss, curling shingles, repeated leaks or soft decking point to a replacement. We show you photos of what we find so you can decide.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most homes take one to three days, depending on size, weather and whether damaged decking has to be replaced.

What should I do after a storm damages my roof?

Stay off the roof, photograph any damage you can see from the ground, cover valuables under active leaks, and call a roofer for an inspection. Contact your insurance company before paying for major work.
