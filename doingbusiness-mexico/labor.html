Labor and employment guide

Mexico's Federal Labor Law sets mandatory benefits, contract rules and how employees can be dismissed. Here is what employers need to know before hiring.

Employment contracts

Employment relationships should be documented in writing. Contracts are indefinite by default. Fixed-term or project contracts are allowed only when the nature of the work justifies them.

Indefinite contracts can include a probation period of up to 30 days, or up to 180 days for management and specialized technical roles.

Mandatory benefits

  • Christmas bonus (aguinaldo): at least 15 days of salary, paid by December 20.
  • Vacation: 12 days after the first year of service, increasing with seniority.
  • Vacation premium: at least 25% of the salary for vacation days.
  • Profit sharing (PTU): 10% of the company's taxable profit, shared among employees, with a cap per employee.
  • Social security: registration with IMSS, plus housing fund (INFONAVIT) and retirement contributions.
  • State payroll tax: rates vary by state.

Outsourcing and staffing

Since the 2021 reform, companies cannot subcontract personnel to carry out their core business activity. Specialized services that are not part of the company's main activity are still allowed, but the provider must be registered in the REPSE registry.

Unions and collective agreements

Following the 2019 labor reform, collective bargaining agreements must be approved by workers in a personal, free and secret vote. Agreements are registered with the Federal Center for Conciliation and Labor Registration. Most labor disputes must go through conciliation before reaching a labor court.

Ending employment

An employee dismissed without justified cause is generally entitled to three months of salary, plus 20 days of salary per year of service in many cases, a seniority premium, and any accrued benefits. Justified dismissals require written notice that states the cause.

Hiring foreign staff

Foreign employees need a visa and residence permit that allows work, issued through the National Migration Institute (INM). The employer must first register with INM to sponsor them.

Common questions

Is there a probation period in Mexico?

Yes. Indefinite contracts can include up to 30 days of probation, or up to 180 days for managers and specialized technical roles.

Can I use a staffing agency?

Only for specialized services outside your core business activity, and only through a provider registered in REPSE.

When is the aguinaldo paid?

By December 20 each year. It is at least 15 days of salary, prorated for employees who worked part of the year.
