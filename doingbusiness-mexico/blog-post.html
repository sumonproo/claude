Annual IMMEX report: what to file and when

Every IMMEX holder files an annual report with the Ministry of Economy. Here is what goes in it.

E1

Expert name

IMMEX and customs · Published November 2026 · 6 min read

Each year, companies with an IMMEX program report their total sales and the value of their exports for the previous year to the Ministry of Economy. The report shows that the company still meets the program's requirements.

What the report includes

The report covers total annual sales and the value of exports in the previous fiscal year. It is filed online through the Ministry of Economy's systems.

When it is due

The report is due in May for the previous year. Missing it can lead to the program being suspended, which stops temporary imports.

Set a reminder in March. Pulling the export figures together takes longer than filing the report.

After you file

Keep the acknowledgment of receipt with your IMMEX records. Customs or the Ministry may ask for it during an audit.
