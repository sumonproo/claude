IMMEX program guide

IMMEX lets manufacturers import raw materials, parts and machinery without paying import duty or VAT up front, as long as the finished goods are exported.

What IMMEX is

IMMEX (Industria Manufacturera, Maquiladora y de Servicios de Exportación) is a program run by the Ministry of Economy. It replaced the separate maquiladora and PITEX programs in 2006.

With an IMMEX program, a company can temporarily import goods used to manufacture, transform or repair products for export. General import duty is not paid on those goods. VAT on the import can also be offset with VAT certification from the tax authority (SAT).

Program types

  • Industrial: one company manufactures or transforms goods for export.
  • Services: repair or service work on goods for export.
  • Holding: a parent company holds the program for its group companies in Mexico.
  • Shelter: a Mexican shelter company holds the program, and a foreign manufacturer operates under it without forming its own company for a limited period.
  • Outsourcing: the program holder has production done by third-party manufacturers.

Requirements

  • A Mexican company with an active tax ID (RFC) and electronic signature (e.firma).
  • A positive tax compliance opinion from SAT.
  • Registration in the importers' registry (Padrón de Importadores).
  • Proof of an export operation, such as contracts or purchase orders with foreign customers.
  • Facilities where the operation takes place, registered with SAT.
  • An automated inventory control system that tracks imported goods (known as Annex 24).

How long imported goods can stay

Raw materials, parts and packaging can generally stay in Mexico for up to 18 months before they must be exported, returned or changed to a definitive import. Machinery and equipment can stay for as long as the program is active.

Keeping the program

IMMEX holders must file an annual report of sales and exports with the Ministry of Economy, keep the inventory control system up to date, and meet the minimum export requirement for their program. Missing these obligations can lead to suspension of the program.

Common questions

Do I need a Mexican company to use IMMEX?

To hold your own program, yes. A shelter arrangement lets you operate under a shelter company's program without forming your own company for a limited period.

Is IMMEX the same as a maquiladora?

IMMEX is the current program. It merged the former maquiladora and PITEX programs in 2006, and people still often call IMMEX companies maquiladoras.

What happens to VAT on temporary imports?

Companies with VAT certification from SAT get a credit equal to the VAT on their temporary imports, so the tax does not tie up cash.
