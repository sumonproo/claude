What kinds of outdoor projects do you do?

Fences and gates, exterior carpentry and dry-rot repair, concrete, pavers and hardscape. If your project is not listed, ask us.

Which materials hold up best near the coast?

It depends on exposure. We choose lumber, finishes and corrosion-resistant fasteners suited to salt air and fog, and will explain the options in your estimate.

Can you combine outdoor work with an interior remodel?

Yes. Many homeowners combine projects, and having one contractor manage both keeps things simple.
