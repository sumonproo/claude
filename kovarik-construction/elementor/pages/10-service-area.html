Local Knowledge

We know Peninsula homes

The Monterey Peninsula has its own mix of homes: older cottages on small lots, mid-century houses, and larger coastal properties. Many are exposed to salt air, fog and moisture, and several of our cities have their own design review and permit processes.

Working locally means we plan for all of this from the start, choosing materials that suit the climate, and scheduling around the realities of each neighborhood.

Where We Work

Communities we serve

Monterey

From New Monterey and Old Town to the Skyline and Del Monte Forest edges, Monterey homes range from historic adobes to post-war ranch houses. We handle kitchen and whole-home remodels, bathroom updates, ADUs and outdoor projects throughout the city.

Carmel-by-the-Sea

Carmel's cottages and small lots call for careful planning and work that respects the village character. We take on bathroom and interior remodels, flooring, tile and exterior carpentry, and plan around the city's design review process.

Pacific Grove

Pacific Grove is known for its Victorian and older homes, which often need updated layouts, bathrooms and repairs behind the walls. We help homeowners modernize while keeping the details that give these houses their charm.

Pebble Beach

For homes in Pebble Beach and the Del Monte Forest, we provide remodeling, bathroom renovations, flooring, tile and outdoor improvements with the level of finish and site care these properties require.

Seaside

Seaside's neighborhoods have many homes that are well suited to garage conversions, ADUs and updated kitchens and baths. We help Seaside homeowners add space and value with practical, well-built projects.
