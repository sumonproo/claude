Do ADUs and garage conversions need a permit? {{ico:chev}}

Yes. In California, ADUs and garage conversions into living space require a building permit from the local city or county. We work with your designer and the building department through that process.

Can my garage be converted into an ADU? {{ico:chev}}

Many garages can. It depends on the structure, the foundation, utilities and local rules. The best first step is to send us photos so we can take a look.

Where do you build ADUs? {{ico:chev}}

Throughout the Monterey Peninsula: Monterey, Carmel-by-the-Sea, Pacific Grove, Pebble Beach and Seaside. Each jurisdiction has its own review process.
