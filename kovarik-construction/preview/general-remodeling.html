General Remodeling

A remodel that fits the way you live

Many Peninsula homes were built decades ago, with small kitchens, closed-off rooms and systems that need updating. A good remodel keeps what you love about your house and fixes what doesn't work.

Kovarik Construction manages general remodeling projects from planning through final clean-up. You get one point of contact, a clear written scope and a team that keeps your home as livable as possible while we work.

What areas do you serve for remodeling projects?

We work throughout the Monterey Peninsula, including Monterey, Carmel-by-the-Sea, Pacific Grove, Pebble Beach and Seaside. See our service area.

How do I get an estimate for a remodel?

Fill out our estimate request form with a short description and a few photos of the space, or call us at (831) 555-0100. We will follow up to talk through your project and arrange a visit.

Can I live at home during a remodel?

Often, yes. It depends on which rooms are affected. We will talk through the schedule with you and plan the work to keep your home as usable as possible.
